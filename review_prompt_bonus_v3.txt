## BONUS — The one move to first place

Read this project as what it is: a VERTICAL medical-safety application whose engine is a counterintuitive, statistically-backed research finding — that adding a judge which DISAGREES MORE with the panel makes the panel MORE truth-anchored (ΔΦ_V rises, with bootstrap confidence intervals), precisely on the stratum where the incumbent judges share a blind spot (silent diagnosis drops). It is NOT a general-purpose developer eval tool, and the team has deliberately chosen to deepen the medical mission rather than pivot to horizontal infrastructure. Assess it on those terms.

Answer three questions in order. Be adversarial on the first before you are constructive on the rest.

Treat "stay medical" as the default: only recommend leaving the domain (a second domain, a general-purpose eval product) if you can make an explicit, affirmative argument that the medical vertical is the wrong lane — and if you do, justify it against the fact that a crowded "evaluate-any-model" tool is a well-populated category.

3. **Top-3 honesty for a Best Apps and Agents track.** In a field of roughly 4,000 submissions, is this credibly top-3 as it stands? Separate two verdicts cleanly: what would make it a strong research artifact versus what would make it a winning APP or AGENT a judge can open and use.
